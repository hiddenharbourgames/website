The Studio

Behind the Studio

Luc

Founder & Game Designer

Games have always been part of my everyday life – and at some point, playing them wasn't enough. I wanted to create my own and add something of my own to the gaming world.

What I love most is the very beginning: coming up with ideas, asking where a game could go and what would make it cool. Then comes the loop I can't get enough of – trying, tinkering, improving, until every piece fits.

As a player, I'm drawn to simulators and strategy games – games where your decisions matter and you have to think ahead.

Hidden Harbour is a one-person studio – for now.
